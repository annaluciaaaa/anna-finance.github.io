My Finance Dashboard

Finance doesn't have to be complicated. Knowing where markets are heading and how to read a few key signals can make a real difference in how you manage your money. This dashboard shows my personal watchlist — the indices, ETFs, stocks, and crypto I actively follow and hold.

RSI (Relative Strength Index)
Measures momentum. Below 30 = oversold, possible buy signal. Above 70 = overbought, possible sell signal.
MACD
Shows when momentum is shifting. A green histogram means upward pressure is building. A cross above the signal line = bullish sign.
Candlestick Chart
Each candle = one time period. Green = price rose, red = price fell. The thin wicks show the high and low of that period.
Fibonacci Retracement
Key price levels based on natural ratios (23.6%, 38.2%, 61.8%). Price often pauses or reverses here — used as support and resistance zones.
Bollinger Bands
Two bands around a moving average. When price touches the upper band it may be overextended. Lower band can signal a bounce.
Line vs. Candles Tab
Both open the full TradingView chart. Use Line for a clean price overview, Candles for detailed OHLC analysis. All indicators are pre-loaded.
